Formats that win on algo
POV reels — massive organic reach
5+ slide carousels — highest saves
Plain text hot takes — comment wars
Stories polls — daily engagement signal
Never post these
"We are excited to announce…" — kills trust
Blue gradient + white text — screams ad
Stock photos of laptops — zero relatability
Long hashtag lists — 3–5 only, targeted
Voice rules
Write like a 21-year-old texting a friend
Use "bro", "literally", "ngl" sparingly
Lowercase > Title Case always
Short sentences. One idea per line.
