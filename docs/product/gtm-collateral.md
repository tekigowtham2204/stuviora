## 1. Placement-cell one-pager (A4, front only)

**Header:** Stuviora logo + "Hire students. Trust the platform."

**Headline:** Paid, real-world work for your students. Free for your college.

**The three things you get (icon row):**
1. **Income + portfolios for students.** Verified students earn from real
   freelance projects and keep 85% of every rupee. Each completed project
   becomes a rated portfolio item.
2. **NEP and NAAC evidence, automatically.** Your private dashboard shows
   cohort signups, active earners, and total earnings: documented
   experiential learning, no paperwork.
3. **Safety you can put your name behind.** College-email verification,
   an AI quality check on every delivery before the client sees it, and
   escrow so no student is ever left unpaid.

**How it works (4 steps):** Student signs up with the college email >
gets matched to real briefs > delivers through the AI quality gate >
is paid from escrow on approval.

**The pilot offer (boxed):** One batch, 30 to 50 students, four weeks,
zero cost. We onboard them and bring the first briefs. At the end you
see the numbers and decide. No contract, no commitment.

## 2. The 8-slide placement-cell deck (outline + key line per slide)

1. **Title.** Stuviora: paid, protected freelance work for your
   students. *"Hire students. Trust the platform."*
2. **The problem.** One in three early freelancers loses money to scams
   or ghosting, and most students graduate with no proof of work.
3. **What Stuviora is.** A student-only marketplace: verified students,
   real briefs from real businesses, 85% to the student.
4. **The trust layer (the slide that closes).** Live demo screenshot of
   the AI gate scoring a delivery. *"Nothing reaches a client unchecked;
   nobody works unpaid."*
5. **What your students get.** Income, a rated portfolio, skill badges,
   protection. One student money-win story with a real payout figure.
6. **What your office gets.** Cohort dashboard screenshot: signups,
   active earners, total earned. Maps to the UGC 60 to 120 hour
   internship mandate and NAAC criteria.
7. **The pilot.** One batch, four weeks, free, we bring the first
   briefs. Success bar stated up front: 40% signup, 10 students paid.
8. **Ask.** Pick the batch and a date. [contact details]
